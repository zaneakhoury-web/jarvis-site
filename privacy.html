Privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Jarvis is a personal app used only by its owner, Zane Khoury. It connects only to Zane's own Google account.

What it accesses

  • Gmail: reads Zane's messages so it can sort them and let him know about important ones; archives messages and sends emails only after Zane approves each action.
  • Google Calendar: read-only, to show Zane his schedule.

How data is stored and shared

  • Data is stored only on Zane's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Short excerpts of an email may be sent to an AI model provider (Anthropic) to summarize it for Zane. They are not used to train models.
  • Google user data is handled in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Local data can be deleted by removing the app's data folder.
